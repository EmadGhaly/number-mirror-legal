Privacy Policy — Number Mirror

Effective date: November 1, 2026
Operator: Number Mirror ("we", "us")
Contact: mobile.development.eny@gmail.com

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the Android app Number Mirror (in Arabic: مرآة الأرقام; package com.eny.numbermirror.ai) handles information. Number Mirror offers numerology for self-reflection and entertainment in Arabic and English. You do not need an account to use it.

This policy covers the app. Third-party services that the app uses — Google (advertising and consent), AI providers, and Google Play — process information under their own privacy policies, which we link below.

2. Summary

  • Your profiles, birth dates and reports are stored on your device. We do not run a server that receives them.
  • If online AI is on (it is on by default), the app sends calculated numbers and your questions to the assistant to an AI provider. It does not send names, Latin spellings or birth dates.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. Google may collect device identifiers and usage information for advertising.
  • You can turn online AI off, manage ad choices where offered, and delete all data in the app at any time.

3. Information you enter

  • Profiles: a display name, an optional Latin spelling of the name, and a date of birth. You can create profiles for yourself and for other people. Please add other people's details only with their knowledge or where you are otherwise allowed to do so.
  • Questions to the AI assistant, which you type.
  • Optional AI provider keys (Google Gemini, Mistral, Groq, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI).
  • Preferences, such as language, appearance, reminder time and focus areas, your personalization settings (explanation length, tone, preferred and excluded topics), your favourites, and optional feedback on results ("helpful / not helpful", "too short", "too long", "not relevant to me").
  • Reports of AI content, if you choose to send one (see section 7).

4. Information created and stored on your device

The app calculates your numbers and stores profiles, reports, daily insights, journey progress, AI results and preferences in the app's private storage on your device. Assistant conversations are kept only for the current session unless you turn on "Save conversations on this device" (off by default).

  • AI provider keys are encrypted using the Android Keystore and are excluded from Android backups.
  • Other app data is stored in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox; it is not separately encrypted.
  • If backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the app's data (except AI keys) in your Google account backup. This backup is managed by Google and your device settings.

Personalization. When "Learn from my feedback" is on (Settings → Personalization), the app uses your feedback, on your device only, to adjust the length of results and the topics it suggests. When "Use my previous insights" is on, it looks at your recent Daily Insights, on your device only, to avoid repeating topics and angles. This is simple rule-based adjustment, not training of any AI model. Your choices are treated as presentation preferences, not as facts about you. You can turn either option off, reset your preferences, delete your feedback, or delete saved insights and reports in Settings → Personalization.

5. Online AI processing

Online AI is optional and can be turned off in Settings → Online AI. When it is off, the app uses built-in offline interpretations only.

When online AI is on, the app sends to the AI provider:

  • your calculated numerology numbers and the type of request, the app language, and short labels for how to present the result (length, tone and angle — for example "short" or "neutral"). Your feedback and history are not sent;
  • for the assistant, your questions and the conversation, after replacing names the app knows with placeholders. If you type other personal details into a question yourself, they are sent as you typed them.

The app does not send birth dates or the Latin spellings you type. It sends a name only in one case: the name contains letters the app can't calculate itself (for example Cyrillic, Greek or Chinese), and you choose Use AI to get a Latin spelling. Then only the name text is sent to the AI provider, and you review the proposed spelling before it is used. The numbers are always calculated on your device. Names in Latin or Arabic letters are never sent.

AI providers:

  • Pollinations — the free built-in option (no key needed).
  • Google (Gemini API), Mistral AI, Groq, Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI — only if you add your own key. If automatic switching is on, the app may use another provider for which you saved a working key when the selected one is unavailable (paid providers only if you allow it).

These providers process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. Some free services may use submitted content to improve their models. For example, Google's terms state that content sent through unpaid Gemini API services may be used to improve Google products and may be reviewed by humans (different rules apply in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK); Mistral's free Experiment plan may use requests for training unless you opt out in your Mistral account. Please review the provider's terms before adding a key:
Pollinations · Gemini API terms · Mistral AI · Groq · Anthropic · OpenAI

AI results are stored on your device so they can be shown again without a new request.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: native ads within its pages and, occasionally, a full-screen (interstitial) ad when you leave a reading you have just completed — never at launch, while you read or type, or more than twice per session. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • device or other identifiers (such as the advertising ID and app set ID);
  • the IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • interactions with ads (for example, views and taps);
  • diagnostic information.

The app does not pass your profiles, numbers, reports or conversations to the ad service.

Consent and choices. In regions where it is required (for example, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Choosing non-personalized ads does not stop all processing: Google may still use information for purposes such as frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy

7. Reporting AI-generated content

Every AI-generated text has a Report button. If you send a report, the app sends: the reported text (which may include names that appear in that text), the reason you chose, your optional note, the AI provider's name, the app language and the app version. It is sent by email, through your own email app, to mobile.development.eny@gmail.com; you can review the message before sending it. We use reports only to review content safety and improve the app, and keep them for 12 months.

8. Notifications

The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your device. It is off by default, and its text contains no personal information. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn the reminder on.

9. Sharing you choose

When you share a report, a daily card or an export (text, PDF, image or Excel), the app opens your device's share sheet. The shared content may include the person's name if you keep that option on. Once shared, the content is handled by the app or person you shared it with.

10. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: online AI, ads, and reconnecting after the network returns.
  • Notifications, start at boot, vibration: the optional daily reminder.
  • Advertising ID and ad services: Google AdMob ads.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or file-storage permissions.

11. How information is used

To provide the app's features (calculations, interpretations, reminders, history, sharing); to answer your AI requests; to show ads and respect your ad choices; and to review reports of AI content. We do not sell personal information. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

12. Who receives information

  • AI providers, only when online AI is on (section 5).
  • Google, for advertising and consent (section 6).
  • Us, only for reports you send (section 7).
  • People or apps you choose when you share.

13. Where information is processed

Your profiles and reports stay on your device. Google and the AI providers may process information in other countries, including the United States, under their own terms.

14. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: data stays until you delete it. You can delete individual items; use Settings → Personalization to delete your feedback, or saved insights, reports, saved conversations and stored AI texts (optionally keeping your feedback); or use Settings → Delete all my data to remove profiles, reports, history, conversations, keys and preferences. Uninstalling the app also removes it from the device.
  • Google and AI providers: information they receive is kept under their own policies; we cannot delete it on their behalf.
  • Reports sent to us: kept for 12 months; contact us to request deletion.

15. Security

Network requests use HTTPS. AI keys are encrypted with the Android Keystore. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

16. Your rights and choices

In the app you can turn online AI off, change ad choices where offered, delete individual items or all data, and turn the reminder off. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because your app data is kept on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app. For reports you sent us, contact us.

17. Children

Number Mirror is not directed to children under 18 and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us.

18. Third-party links

The app may open third-party websites, such as AI providers' key pages and Google Play. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

19. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify users in the app or on the store listing.
